BambooHR is built for small-to-mid-market companies that want HR, time tracking, and basic recruiting in one platform. It does the core job well — employee records, PTO, onboarding — but its recruiting module is intentionally lightweight. That is a feature, not a bug, as long as you connect the right tools to fill the gaps.

This guide covers 10 BambooHR integrations across five categories that extend it into a full hiring stack. If you recruit in Greenhouse and run HR in BambooHR, our [Greenhouse integrations guide](/blog/tools-that-integrate-with-greenhouse) covers the recruiting side, and our guide to [improving your hiring process](/blog/how-to-improve-recruitment-process) covers the process itself.

## How BambooHR integrations expand its hiring tools

BambooHR's [applicant tracking](https://www.bamboohr.com/platform/applicant-tracking-system/) handles job postings, application collection, and basic pipeline management. Where it stops short (as of September 2026):

- **No AI screening.** Applications arrive and sit until someone reads them.
- **No built-in assessments.** Skills tests and take-homes require a separate tool.
- **No outbound sourcing.** BambooHR collects inbound applicants but does not help you find people who have not applied.
- **Lighter analytics.** Standard hiring reports, with less depth on stage-by-stage conversion than a dedicated ATS.

Integrations solve these gaps without replacing BambooHR as the HR system of record. Candidate data flows from specialised tools into BambooHR's pipeline, and hired employees flow into BambooHR's HR module automatically.

## Sourcing integrations

### 1. Fluxberry

Fluxberry is an AI-native hiring platform that scores every applicant against your job requirements the moment they apply. For BambooHR users, it replaces the manual resume-reading step — you open a ranked shortlist with scores, summaries, and reasoning instead of an unfiltered pile.

**How it connects:** There is no native BambooHR integration yet. Fluxberry runs its own pipeline and careers page, and sends a signed webhook when a candidate is shortlisted, changes stage or is hired. Route those events into BambooHR with an automation tool such as Zapier or Make, or a short script against BambooHR's API, and your shortlist reaches BambooHR without anyone rereading resumes.

**Why it fits BambooHR teams:**
- Small teams using BambooHR often have no dedicated recruiter, and AI scoring takes the first pass through every application so nobody has to read the whole pile
- A built-in careers page, custom application forms, and shared notes for your team reduce the number of additional tools needed
- Pipeline stages are customisable to mirror your BambooHR workflow

### 2. Indeed

Indeed is the largest job board globally and offers a direct integration with BambooHR. Job posts published in BambooHR distribute to Indeed automatically, and applications flow back into the BambooHR pipeline.

**Best for:** Teams that rely on job board volume and want applicants to land directly in BambooHR without manual import.

## Assessment integrations

### 3. Criteria Corp

Criteria provides pre-employment aptitude, personality, and skills assessments. Its BambooHR integration sends assessment invitations when candidates reach a specific pipeline stage and writes results back to the candidate record.

**Best for:** Teams hiring for roles where cognitive ability, personality fit, or specific aptitudes matter — sales, customer support, operations.

### 4. eSkill

eSkill offers customisable skills tests across hundreds of job functions — typing, software proficiency, industry knowledge, and more. Integration with BambooHR triggers tests automatically and surfaces results alongside the candidate profile.

**Best for:** High-volume hiring for roles with measurable technical or administrative skills — data entry, accounting, call centre.

## Background check integrations

### 5. Checkr

Checkr provides fast, API-driven background checks with a clean candidate experience. Its BambooHR integration initiates checks from within the pipeline and posts results back when complete. Turnaround is typically one to three business days.

**Best for:** Growing companies that need background checks at scale with quick turnaround and a modern candidate experience.

### 6. GoodHire

GoodHire offers background checks with built-in compliance guidance — state-specific adverse action letters, FCRA workflows, and ban-the-box compliance. Its BambooHR integration is straightforward: trigger a check, get results in the candidate record.

**Best for:** Small companies without in-house legal or compliance teams who want background checks that handle the regulatory complexity for them.

## Communication and offer integrations

### 7. DocuSign

DocuSign integrates with BambooHR to handle offer letters and employment agreements electronically. Templates pull candidate data from BambooHR, send for e-signature, and file the signed document back to the employee record.

**Best for:** Companies that already run contracts through DocuSign across the business. BambooHR's own [offer templates include e-signatures](https://www.bamboohr.com/platform/applicant-tracking-system/), so for offer letters alone you may not need it.

### 8. Slack

Slack's BambooHR integration provides notification-level connectivity — new hire announcements, PTO alerts, and work anniversary reminders posted to channels. For recruiting, the main value is keeping hiring managers informed of pipeline updates without logging into BambooHR.

**Best for:** Teams that live in Slack and want hiring updates (new applicant, candidate advanced, offer accepted) surfaced in channels alongside other work.

## Payroll and benefits integrations

### 9. Gusto

Gusto handles payroll, benefits, and HR for small companies. Its BambooHR integration syncs employee data bidirectionally — new hires added in BambooHR flow into Gusto for payroll setup, and compensation changes sync back.

**Best for:** Small companies (under 100 employees) using BambooHR for HR and Gusto for payroll who want to eliminate double data entry.

### 10. TriNet

TriNet is a professional employer organisation (PEO) that bundles payroll, benefits, and compliance for small and mid-size businesses. Its BambooHR integration syncs employee records, benefits elections, and payroll data.

**Best for:** Companies using a PEO model who want BambooHR as their HR system of record while TriNet handles payroll and benefits administration.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does BambooHR have built-in AI screening?

Not as of September 2026. BambooHR's applicant tracking collects and organises applications, but it does not score, rank, or summarise them. Tools like Fluxberry add an AI screening layer that works alongside BambooHR's pipeline.

### Can I use Fluxberry as my main ATS instead of BambooHR's recruiting module?

Yes. Fluxberry covers the full hiring workflow: sourcing on the Max plan, AI screening, interviews on your Google Calendar, and offers. When someone is hired, its `candidate.hired` webhook can hand them to BambooHR for onboarding through an automation tool. You get a purpose-built ATS while BambooHR stays your HR system. See how it compares in our guide to the [best applicant tracking systems for small businesses](/blog/best-applicant-tracking-systems).

### How many integrations does BambooHR support?

BambooHR's [marketplace](https://www.bamboohr.com/integrations/) lists over 150 integrations across recruiting, payroll, benefits, time tracking, and performance management. The quality and depth of each integration varies — some are bidirectional syncs, others are one-way data pushes.
