Metaview started as an AI note-taker for interviews and has grown into what it calls an agentic recruiting platform, with separate agents for [sourcing, application review and interview notes](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing). It works on top of your ATS rather than replacing it, and for teams that run a lot of interviews it solves a real problem.

But it is not the only way to get AI into your hiring. If you are evaluating Metaview and wondering whether the money is better spent elsewhere, or you use it and need tools that cover what it does not, this guide breaks down nine Metaview alternatives by what they actually replace.

## Why teams look for Metaview alternatives

Three patterns come up repeatedly:

1. **They would rather have one system than two.** Metaview adds AI to an ATS; it is not one. If you would rather screen, track and hire in the same place, an ATS with AI built in removes a tool, a login and an integration to maintain.

2. **Per-seat pricing adds up.** Metaview prices its paid plans per user. Its AI sourcing plans, for example, start at $100 per user per month on its [pricing page](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing) (as of September 2026). A small team hiring for a few roles a year pays for seats it rarely uses.

3. **They only need one piece of it.** If the real gap is interview documentation, a dedicated interview intelligence tool, or the AI already in your video platform, may cover it for less.

## Interview intelligence and meeting tools

### 2. BrightHire

BrightHire records interviews, generates transcripts, and creates structured highlights tied to your scorecard. Of everything on this list it is the closest to Metaview's note-taking: same category, different execution. [Zoom acquired BrightHire](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoom-acquires-brighthire/) in December 2025 and runs it as a standalone brand.

**What it adds over Metaview:** Interviewer coaching features, compliance-focused recording consent flows, and a path into Zoom Workplace if your interviews already happen there.

**Best for:** Mid-size teams with a formal interview process who want structured evidence alongside their scorecards.

### 3. Employ AI Interview Companion (formerly Pillar)

Pillar's interview intelligence now lives on as the [AI Interview Companion](https://www.employinc.com/ai-interview-companion/) from Employ, the company behind Lever, Jobvite and JazzHR. It records and summarises interviews, and flags inconsistent scoring and potentially biased language for coaching.

**Best for:** Teams already on an Employ ATS who want interview intelligence from the same vendor.

### 4. Fireflies.ai

Fireflies is a general-purpose meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and summarises any meeting — not just interviews. It integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams, and offers a searchable transcript library.

**What it replaces:** Metaview's core recording and note-taking, but for every meeting type. The tradeoff is that it is not built specifically for hiring, so you lose recruiting-specific features like scorecard alignment.

**Best for:** Teams that want one tool for all meetings, not just interviews.

### 5. Zoom AI Companion

If your interviews happen on Zoom, the built-in [AI Companion](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-assistant/) generates summaries and action items without an additional tool. It is included with paid Zoom plans.

**What it replaces:** The basic note-taking function of Metaview — no third-party integration required.

**Best for:** Teams already paying for Zoom who want zero-setup meeting summaries.

### 6. Otter.ai

Otter provides real-time transcription and searchable meeting archives. It sits between Fireflies and Zoom AI Companion in terms of specialisation — more powerful than built-in tools, less recruiting-specific than BrightHire.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious teams who need accurate transcription across all meetings.

## Async video screening

### 7. Hireflix

[Hireflix](https://hireflix.com/) replaces live first-round interviews with one-way video questions. Candidates record answers on their own time, and you review them asynchronously. This eliminates the scheduling problem entirely for early-stage screening.

**What it replaces:** The interviews Metaview would have recorded. If the conversation does not need to be live, it does not need real-time note-taking either.

**Best for:** High-volume roles where a 15-minute live screen for every candidate is not feasible.

## ATS-native options

### 8. Ashby

Ashby is a modern ATS with built-in analytics, scheduling, structured interviewing and AI-assisted application review. Its native interview scorecards and pipeline analytics reduce the need for bolt-on tools like Metaview. Already on Ashby? See the [tools that integrate with Ashby](/blog/tools-that-integrate-with-ashby).

**Best for:** Teams choosing a new ATS who want interview management built in rather than bolted on.

### 9. Greenhouse

Greenhouse supports structured hiring out of the box — interview kits, scorecards, and a marketplace of over 400 integrations. Many teams find that Greenhouse's native features plus one or two [Greenhouse integrations](/blog/tools-that-integrate-with-greenhouse) cover what Metaview does.

**Best for:** Scaling teams that want a mature, extensible ATS with strong interviewing support.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Metaview worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you already have an ATS you like. Metaview adds AI sourcing, application review and interview notes on top of an ATS, priced per user. If you would rather have screening inside the ATS, Fluxberry covers screening, the pipeline and, on Max, sourcing from {{price:pro:monthly}}/month for the whole team.

### Can I use Metaview with any ATS?

Metaview integrates with most major ATS platforms including Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby. However, the depth of integration varies. Check whether the features you need (scorecard sync, candidate linking) are supported for your specific ATS.

### What is the cheapest Metaview alternative?

For interview notes, Zoom AI Companion costs nothing extra on paid Zoom plans and covers basic meeting summaries. For screening and the ATS together, Fluxberry starts at {{price:pro:monthly}}/month and [covers](/sign-up) AI scoring, pipeline management, and a careers page in one tool.

### Do I need interview recording if I have AI screening?

Not necessarily. AI screening (like Fluxberry's applicant scoring) reduces the number of interviews you conduct by surfacing the best candidates earlier. Fewer interviews means less note-taking to manage. Many small teams find that better screening is enough, and add a note-taker only when interview volume grows.
