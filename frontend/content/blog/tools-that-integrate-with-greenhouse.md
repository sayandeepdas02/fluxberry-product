## How Greenhouse integrations work

Greenhouse works best as the centre of your hiring workflow — the system of record for candidates, stages, and decisions. Integrations feed into it (sourcing tools push candidates in) and act on it (assessment tools trigger when candidates reach a stage), but Greenhouse owns the pipeline.

Three characteristics of a strong Greenhouse integration:

1. **Bidirectional data sync.** Candidates and activity data flow both directions. A sourcing tool pushes candidates into Greenhouse; stage changes in Greenhouse trigger actions in the external tool.
2. **Minimal manual overhead.** If you have to copy-paste data between tools, the integration is not doing its job.
3. **Greenhouse stays the single view.** Recruiters and hiring managers should not need to check a separate dashboard for screening results, assessment scores, or background check status.

## Sourcing integrations

### 1. Fluxberry

Fluxberry is an AI-native hiring platform that scores every applicant against your job requirements the moment they apply. Greenhouse now has AI matching of its own: [Talent Matching, part of Greenhouse Real Talent](https://www.greenhouse.com/real-talent-candidate-matching), prioritises applicants against calibrations a recruiter sets. Fluxberry works before Greenhouse instead, on its own careers page and pipeline, so by the time a recruiter opens Greenhouse every candidate has a score, a written summary, and the reasoning behind it.

**How it connects:** There is no native Greenhouse integration yet. Fluxberry runs its own pipeline and careers page, and sends a signed webhook when a candidate is shortlisted, changes stage or is hired. Route those events into Greenhouse with an automation tool such as Zapier or Make, or a short script against Greenhouse's API, and your shortlist reaches Greenhouse without anyone rereading resumes.

**Why it fits Greenhouse teams:**
- Screening happens before Greenhouse, so your Greenhouse setup, interview kits and scorecards stay exactly as they are
- Every score comes with a summary, strengths and concerns, so reviewers can see why a candidate ranked where they did
- Analytics complement Greenhouse's reporting with channel attribution and drop-off analysis

### 2. LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect

LinkedIn RSC syncs InMail activity, profile views, and candidate interest signals between LinkedIn Recruiter and Greenhouse. Candidates sourced on LinkedIn appear in the Greenhouse pipeline automatically.

**Best for:** Teams with LinkedIn Recruiter seats who want sourcing activity reflected in Greenhouse without manual candidate creation.

### 3. Gem

Gem layers sourcing, outreach sequences, and analytics on top of LinkedIn and email. Its Greenhouse integration syncs all touchpoints — emails sent, opens, replies — back to the candidate record, and pushes sourced candidates directly into Greenhouse.

**Best for:** Sourcing-heavy teams that run multi-step outreach campaigns and want complete attribution data in Greenhouse.

## Scheduling integrations

### 4. GoodTime

GoodTime automates interview scheduling with intelligent interviewer selection, load balancing, and panel coordination. Its Greenhouse integration reads interview plans from Greenhouse, books slots automatically, and updates the event in Greenhouse when confirmed.

**Best for:** Teams with complex, multi-round interview processes where scheduling coordination adds days to the timeline.

### 5. Calendly

Calendly provides self-serve scheduling — candidates pick a time from available slots. Its Greenhouse integration generates unique scheduling links per interview stage and creates Greenhouse events when candidates book.

**Best for:** Teams with simpler scheduling needs (one-on-one screens, first-round calls) who want to eliminate the email back-and-forth.

## Assessment integrations

### 6. HackerRank

HackerRank provides standardised coding challenges and technical interviews. Its Greenhouse integration sends assessment links when candidates reach a designated stage and writes scores, percentile rankings, and code submissions back to the candidate profile.

**Best for:** Engineering hiring at any scale — from startups screening 50 applicants to enterprises running thousands of technical assessments per quarter.

### 7. Codility

Codility offers coding tests with anti-cheating measures, task-based assessments, and live technical interviews. Its Greenhouse integration works similarly to HackerRank — stage-triggered invitations, automated scoring, and results synced to the candidate record.

**Best for:** Teams that want more control over assessment design, including custom tasks and weighted scoring rubrics.

## Background check integrations

### 8. Checkr

Checkr provides fast, API-first background checks with a modern candidate experience. Its Greenhouse integration initiates checks from within the pipeline and posts results to the candidate record. Typical turnaround is one to three business days for standard checks.

**Best for:** Fast-moving teams that need background checks without bottlenecking the offer stage. Checkr's speed and candidate experience are its differentiators.

### 9. Sterling

Sterling offers comprehensive background screening with global coverage. Its Greenhouse integration supports complex screening packages — criminal records, employment verification, education verification, drug testing — across 200+ countries.

**Best for:** Enterprise and regulated-industry teams that need thorough, globally compliant background checks with audit trail documentation.

## HRIS integrations

### 10. Workday

Workday's Greenhouse integration syncs hired candidates from Greenhouse into Workday for onboarding, payroll, and employee lifecycle management. Requisition data can also flow from Workday into Greenhouse, keeping headcount planning and recruiting aligned.

**Best for:** Enterprise organisations using Workday as their HRIS who want a seamless recruit-to-hire-to-onboard pipeline.

### 11. BambooHR

BambooHR's Greenhouse integration syncs new hires from Greenhouse into BambooHR's employee records, triggering onboarding workflows, benefits enrollment, and IT provisioning. For the tools that connect to BambooHR itself, see our guide to [BambooHR integrations](/blog/tools-that-integrate-with-bamboohr).

**Best for:** Small-to-mid-size companies using Greenhouse for recruiting and BambooHR for HR that want to eliminate manual data entry at the point of hire.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Greenhouse have built-in AI screening?

Yes. Since June 2025, [Greenhouse Real Talent](https://www.greenhouse.com/product-features/greenhouse-real-talent) has included Talent Matching, which matches resumes against recruiter-defined, weighted calibrations and explains each recommendation, alongside spam and fraud detection. A separate tool such as Fluxberry makes sense when you want screening to happen before candidates reach Greenhouse, with a score, summary, strengths and concerns for every applicant.

### How do I choose between Greenhouse integrations in the same category?

Evaluate three things: integration depth (bidirectional vs. one-way), pricing at your scale, and whether the tool solves your specific bottleneck. Two scheduling tools may look similar on paper, but one handles panel coordination while the other only does one-on-one booking.

### Can I use Fluxberry without Greenhouse?

Yes. Fluxberry is a standalone AI-native ATS with its own pipeline, careers page, and analytics.
