## How Ashby integrations extend the platform

Ashby already covers more ground natively than most ATS platforms — built-in scheduling, structured scorecards, custom workflows, advanced analytics, and AI-assisted application review. This means integrations should extend Ashby into areas it intentionally does not cover, rather than duplicating features it already has.

Three areas where integrations add the most value:

1. **Upstream sourcing and screening.** Ashby manages candidates from pipeline entry onward. Tools that find people who have not applied yet, or rank applicants before they enter the pipeline, add the most.
2. **Specialised assessment.** Ashby's native scorecards handle interview evaluation, but standardised skills tests and coding challenges require dedicated platforms.
3. **Post-offer handoff.** Moving hired candidates from Ashby into HRIS and payroll systems eliminates the manual data entry that slows onboarding.

## Sourcing integrations

### 1. Fluxberry

Fluxberry is an AI-native hiring platform that scores every applicant against your job requirements on arrival. Ashby has AI screening of its own, [AI-Assisted Application Review](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/ai-assisted-application-review), which marks each resume as meeting or not meeting criteria you define. Ashby says its AI never gives applicants a numerical rating; you can sort by the share of criteria met, but there is no overall score. Fluxberry gives one: every applicant gets a score, a written summary, strengths and concerns, so you start from a ranked shortlist.

**How it connects:** There is no native Ashby integration yet. Fluxberry runs its own pipeline and careers page, and sends a signed webhook when a candidate is shortlisted, changes stage or is hired. Route those events into Ashby with an automation tool such as Zapier or Make, or a short script against Ashby's API, and your shortlist reaches Ashby without anyone rereading resumes. Ashby takes over from pipeline management onward, while Fluxberry handles the screening that happens before.

**Why it fits Ashby teams:**
- Ashby users tend to be opinionated about tooling — Fluxberry's focused, no-bloat approach matches that mindset
- AI scoring eliminates manual resume triage without adding another enterprise platform
- Custom application forms and a careers page are included, so there is one fewer tool to manage
- Analytics on sources, funnel drop-off and time to hire complement Ashby's native reporting

### 2. hireEZ

hireEZ (formerly Hiretual) is an AI sourcing platform that searches across 45+ data sources to find passive candidates. Its Ashby integration pushes sourced candidates into the pipeline with sourcing context (where they were found, match reasoning) attached.

**Best for:** Teams doing heavy outbound sourcing for hard-to-fill roles — engineering, data science, niche technical positions — who want sourcing activity reflected in Ashby.

## Assessment integrations

### 3. HackerRank

HackerRank provides standardised coding challenges, real-world problem simulations, and live technical interviews. Its Ashby integration triggers assessments at designated pipeline stages and syncs scores, code submissions, and percentile rankings back to the candidate profile.

**Best for:** Engineering-heavy teams hiring software developers, data engineers, and technical roles where coding ability must be verified before the interview stage.

### 4. CodeSignal

CodeSignal offers AI-powered coding assessments, certified evaluations, and a General Coding Assessment (GCA) score that benchmarks candidates against a global population. Its Ashby integration automates test delivery and results collection.

**Best for:** Teams that want a standardised technical benchmark — CodeSignal's GCA score is used by enough companies that some candidates already have a score on file, reducing assessment friction.

## Background check integrations

### 5. Checkr

Checkr provides fast, API-driven background checks. Its Ashby integration initiates checks from within the pipeline when candidates reach the offer stage, and posts results (pass, consider, or flagged) directly to the candidate record.

**Best for:** Fast-growing startups and tech companies that need background checks without adding days to the offer timeline. Checkr's speed and developer-friendly approach match Ashby's ethos.

### 6. Sterling

Sterling provides comprehensive employment screening with global coverage — criminal records, employment verification, education checks, and drug testing across 200+ countries. Its Ashby integration supports complex screening packages for companies with international hiring needs.

**Best for:** Companies hiring globally or in regulated industries that need thorough screening with compliance documentation and audit trails.

## HRIS integrations

### 7. Rippling

Rippling unifies HR, IT, and finance — employee records, payroll, benefits, device management, and app provisioning. Its Ashby integration syncs hired candidates into Rippling for onboarding, automatically triggering laptop ordering, account creation, and payroll setup.

**Best for:** Tech companies that use Rippling for IT and HR and want the hire-to-onboard transition to be fully automated.

### 8. Workday

Workday's Ashby integration handles the handoff from recruiting to employee lifecycle management — hired candidates flow into Workday for onboarding, payroll, and compensation. Requisition data can sync from Workday into Ashby to keep headcount planning and recruiting aligned.

**Best for:** Larger organisations using Workday as their HRIS that chose Ashby specifically for its modern recruiting experience.

## Communication and collaboration integrations

### 9. Slack

Slack's Ashby integration posts pipeline notifications to designated channels — new applicants, stage changes, offer acceptances, and hiring updates. Hiring managers stay informed without logging into Ashby, and team discussions about candidates happen alongside other work.

**Best for:** Any Ashby team that uses Slack. The integration is lightweight but eliminates the "let me check Ashby" delay when hiring managers need a status update.

### 10. Zoom

Zoom's Ashby integration generates unique meeting links for every scheduled interview and attaches recordings to the candidate record after the call ends. It removes the manual step of creating and sharing meeting links for every interview.

**Best for:** Teams conducting video interviews who want automatic link generation and recording attached to the Ashby candidate profile.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Ashby have built-in AI screening?

Yes. Ashby's [AI-Assisted Application Review](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/ai-assisted-application-review) checks each resume against criteria you set and marks it "Meets" or "Does not meet" for each one. Ashby says the AI never gives applicants a numerical rating, and a person makes every decision, though you can sort applicants by the percentage of criteria they meet. If you want a ranked shortlist with a score and written reasoning for every applicant, that is where a tool like Fluxberry fits.

### Is Ashby's integration ecosystem large enough?

Ashby lists 250+ integrations, fewer than Greenhouse or Lever, and its API-first design lets you build what is missing. For most teams, the key categories — sourcing, assessment, background checks, HRIS, and communication — are well covered. If you need a niche tool, check whether it supports Ashby's API or can connect through Zapier.
