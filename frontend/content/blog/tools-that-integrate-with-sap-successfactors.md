SAP SuccessFactors handles core HR and talent management for large organisations, but its recruiting module works best when connected to specialised tools that extend its reach. The right integrations fill gaps in sourcing, assessment, compliance, and document management — without replacing the system of record.

This guide covers 12 tools across six categories that integrate with SuccessFactors, starting with sourcing and working through the hiring workflow.

## How integrations extend SAP SuccessFactors recruiting

SuccessFactors is built as a platform. Its strength is managing the end-to-end employee lifecycle — requisitions, approvals, onboarding, learning, compensation. But recruiting-specific capabilities like outbound sourcing, technical assessment, and AI-powered screening are better served by tools designed for those problems.

A strong SuccessFactors integration should:

- **Sync candidate data bidirectionally.** Candidates discovered in an external tool should flow into SuccessFactors requisitions without manual re-entry.
- **Respect the system of record.** SuccessFactors owns the requisition and the hire record. Integrations should write to it, not compete with it.
- **Support enterprise compliance.** Data residency, audit trails, and role-based access matter when you operate across regions.

### 2. LinkedIn Recruiter

LinkedIn Recruiter connects to SuccessFactors through Recruiter System Connect (RSC), syncing InMail activity, candidate profiles, and job postings between the two platforms.

**Best for:** Enterprise teams with LinkedIn Recruiter licenses who want sourcing activity visible in SuccessFactors without tab-switching.

### 3. Skima AI

Skima uses AI to search across internal databases and external profiles, matching candidates to requisitions. Its SuccessFactors integration pushes matched candidates directly into the recruiting pipeline.

**Best for:** Large organisations with existing talent databases that want AI matching layered on top of SuccessFactors.

### 4. Eightfold AI

Eightfold's talent intelligence platform analyses candidate data at scale, providing matching, internal mobility insights, and diversity analytics. It connects to SuccessFactors as a pre-processing layer for candidate pipelines.

**Best for:** Enterprise organisations focused on talent intelligence, internal mobility, and workforce planning alongside recruiting.

## Assessment integrations

### 5. HackerRank

HackerRank provides technical coding assessments and interviews. Its SuccessFactors integration sends assessment invitations when candidates reach the evaluation stage and writes results back to the candidate record.

**Best for:** Engineering and technical hiring where standardised coding tests are part of the process.

### 6. HireVue

HireVue combines video interviewing with game-based and coding assessments. Its enterprise integration with SuccessFactors is SAP-certified, supporting multi-language assessments and global compliance requirements.

**Best for:** High-volume enterprise hiring with structured video screening across regions and languages.

## Background check integrations

### 7. Sterling

Sterling is one of the largest background screening providers globally. Its SuccessFactors integration initiates checks from within the recruiting module and posts results back to the candidate record, with full audit trail support.

**Best for:** Enterprise organisations that need background checks across multiple countries with consistent compliance documentation.

### 8. HireRight

HireRight provides employment screening, drug testing, and credential verification. Like Sterling, it integrates with SuccessFactors at the offer stage, triggering checks automatically and returning results to the candidate record.

**Best for:** Organisations in regulated industries (finance, healthcare, government) where screening requirements are strict and auditable.

## Adjacent SuccessFactors module integrations

### 9. SuccessFactors Onboarding

SuccessFactors Onboarding is a native module — not a third-party integration — but it is worth mentioning because many organisations purchase Recruiting and Onboarding separately. When connected, a hired candidate flows directly from the recruiting pipeline into onboarding tasks, paperwork, and provisioning.

**Best for:** Any SuccessFactors Recruiting customer who handles onboarding in a separate system or spreadsheet.

### 10. SuccessFactors Learning

SuccessFactors Learning connects training and certification management to the talent lifecycle. When integrated with Recruiting, it can trigger pre-boarding learning paths for new hires or surface internal candidates with specific certifications.

**Best for:** Organisations where certifications, compliance training, or skills development are tied to hiring and mobility decisions.

## Document and signature integrations

### 11. DocuSign

DocuSign integrates with SuccessFactors to manage offer letters, employment agreements, and onboarding paperwork. Documents are generated from SuccessFactors data and sent for e-signature without leaving the platform.

**Best for:** Any organisation sending offer letters through SuccessFactors that wants to eliminate manual document handling.

### 12. Adobe Sign

Adobe Sign (now Adobe Acrobat Sign) provides similar e-signature functionality with tighter integration into the broader Adobe ecosystem. Its SuccessFactors connector supports templates, routing, and audit trails for employment documents.

**Best for:** Organisations already invested in the Adobe ecosystem that want native document signing in their HR workflow.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does SAP SuccessFactors have built-in sourcing?

SuccessFactors Recruiting supports job posting distribution and internal job boards, but it does not include outbound sourcing, AI-powered candidate matching, or automated screening. These capabilities come from integrations with tools like Fluxberry, LinkedIn Recruiter, or Eightfold.

### How do SuccessFactors integrations handle data privacy?

SAP provides a data integration framework with role-based access, audit logging, and data residency controls. Third-party integrations that are SAP-certified must comply with these standards. Always verify that a tool meets your specific regional requirements (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) before connecting it.

### Can I use Fluxberry without replacing SuccessFactors?

Yes. Fluxberry works alongside SuccessFactors — it handles the sourcing, screening, and assessment work, then pushes qualified candidates into your SuccessFactors pipeline. SuccessFactors stays the system of record for requisitions, approvals, and hire records.
