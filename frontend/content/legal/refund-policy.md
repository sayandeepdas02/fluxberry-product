This Refund Policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for purchases from Fluxberry ("we", "us"). It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, so your receipt and any refund come from Dodo Payments.

## Subscriptions (Pro and Max)

- **Cancel anytime.** You can cancel a monthly or yearly subscription at any time from the Upgrade page in your dashboard. Cancelling stops the next renewal.
- **Access until the end of the period.** After you cancel, you keep full access to your plan until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. Your plan then moves to the free tier.
- **No refunds for partial periods.** Subscription payments, including yearly payments and renewals, are not refundable, and we do not give refunds or credits for unused time, for downgrading, or for cancelling part-way through a period.

## AI credit top-up packs

- Top-up packs (250, 500 or 1,000 credits) are **non-refundable** once purchased.
- Purchased credits **do not expire** while your account is open, so you can use them whenever you need them.
- The monthly credit allowance included with your plan is not a purchase. It resets each month and cannot be refunded or exchanged for money.
- If an AI lookup fails or finds no result, the credits it used are returned to your balance automatically.

## When we always refund

We will refund you in full if:

- you were charged twice for the same purchase;
- you were charged after cancelling, for a period you did not use;
- a billing error on our side charged you the wrong amount;
- we end your access to Fluxberry without cause before the end of a period you paid for, in which case we refund the unused part of that period.

## How to ask for a refund

Email [sayandeep@fluxberry.in](mailto:sayandeep@fluxberry.in) within 30 days of the charge, with the email address on your account and the date and amount of the charge. We will reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Dodo Payments and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
